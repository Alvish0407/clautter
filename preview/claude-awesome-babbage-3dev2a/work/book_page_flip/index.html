About

An open book with justified text from The Book of Tea. Drag anywhere on the right page to start a fold — the page curls along the perpendicular bisector between the corner and your finger. The folded triangle reveals the next page underneath, and the turned flap shows a reflected mirror of the page content. Includes fold-line shadow, highlight, and a bottom slider for quick navigation.

GeometryCustomPaintInteractiveAnimation

Techniques

  • Perpendicular-bisector fold geometry
  • Polygon clipping (fold region computation)
  • Matrix4 reflection transform
  • GestureDetector pan (drag-to-fold)
  • Fold-line shadow & highlight rendering
  • Snap-back / complete animation with curves

Built with Claude × Flutter. It’s open source, and more are on the way.
